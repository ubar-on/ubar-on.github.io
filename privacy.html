Privacy policy

daily-review is used only by its owner.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's Google Tasks (scope tasks.readonly).
  • Task data is used only to build the owner's private morning summary on the owner's own server.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or sent to third parties.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: via github.com/ubar-on.
